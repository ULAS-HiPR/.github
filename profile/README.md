## 🚀 High Powered Rocketry | ULAS

Our mission at the University of Limerick Aeronautical Society (ULAS) | High Powered Rocketry Team (HiPR) is to develop the necessary skills and knowledge to design and operate high powered rockets in order to continue our personal development and prepare us for our future careers.

### 🪓 Ongoing Projects:
- [Ogma V2](): Our SRAD, fully custom flight computer family, including the main computer, the propulsion board, and many other modules.
- [HiPR Deck](): Our bespoke ground station, to receive and send data on the ground.
- [Faigh](): Rack style, fully modular, DAQ and control system, used for anything from liquid test stands, to the filling the rocket at the launch rail.
- [Liquid Propulsion Design](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/Liquid-Propulsion-Design) : Our new, standardized design repo used to design and size liquid propulsion systems (propellant analysis, engine design, feed systems & tanks sizing)

### ✅ Finished Projects:
 - Ogma V1: The first iteration of our SRAD flight computer, now retired. Includes the following boards:
     1. [Croi](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/croi): The main flight board, housing all our sensors.
     2. [Foinse](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/foinse): Power board, sending logic and servo power through the whole stack.
     3. [Lamh](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/lamh): Servo board, capable of controlling 4 servos simultaneously.
     4. [Pleasc](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/pleasc): Pyro board, with 4 pyro channels for all our deployment needs.
     5. [Teachtaire](https://github.com/ULAS-HiPR/teachtaire): GNSS and GPS board, providing live telemtry back to the rocket, and accurate location tracking.
